Referee report

Reviewer 2

Manuscript: "Pedal or Perish Trip #6: Battery to the Backstretch, Lower Manhattan to Saratoga Springs"
Reviewer history: Ohio to Erie (reject), GAP/C&O (accept, minor), this one
Recommendation: Reject and resubmit

This reviewer has seen these authors before. In 2025 this reviewer recommended rejection of their Ohio to Erie proposal and set out five conditions for resubmission. In 2026 this reviewer was replaced by a more agreeable colleague who accepted the GAP/C&O manuscript with minor revisions. That colleague has been reassigned. The concerns from 2025 are reproduced below, together with the authors' progress on each. There has been little.

1. Excessive daily mileage

In the 2025 round this reviewer asked the authors to reduce daily mileage to reasonable levels, defined explicitly as 65 miles per day or fewer. The present manuscript proposes:

  • Day 2 at 72 miles, over the stated ceiling, on what the authors themselves call the first trip with "real hills"
  • Day 3 at 61 miles immediately after, with no recovery day
  • Day lengths that the authors' own route map reports as longer than the figures in the text

This reviewer is aware that the authors rode the 94-mile day in 2025 against this reviewer's advice and now refer to it as "the legendary one." This reviewer does not regard survivorship as a methodology.

2. Availability window

In 2025 the proposed window was compressed between a co-author's triathlon and another co-author's conference in Taiwan, and this reviewer described it as jeopardizing the entire expedition. The authors have addressed the compressed-window problem by not proposing a window at all. The manuscript lists "When: week TBD." The authors further concede that the trip's title depends on horse racing at Saratoga, that the 2027 race dates are unpublished, and that there is no June racing there in 2027. A study whose central outcome may not occur during the study period is not a study. It is a hope.

3. Logistical deficiencies

The 2025 proposal began with a 6-hour drive followed immediately by cycling, and returned on a single vehicle driven by an unspecified "wife." The present proposal begins with a drive to Saratoga Springs, followed by a 3.5- to 4-hour train ride to Manhattan, followed by cycling. The authors have replaced the unspecified wife with Amtrak. This reviewer considers the wife the more reliable instrument. In addition:

  • Bike space on the train is limited and requires a reservation the authors cannot make, because there are no dates
  • At least one co-author's bicycle is described only as "how it gets back from Saratoga"
  • Day 5 depends on a trail the authors concede has "closures with no notice online"

4. Methodological inconsistencies

In 2025 the authors presented two contradictory itineraries, Cleveland-to-Cincinnati and Cincinnati-to-Cleveland, without reconciling them. The present authors have refined this approach. They first submitted a Buffalo-to-Albany canal route, voted for it, then withdrew it and submitted a different river. Within the present manuscript they propose four separate starting points: a harbor photo at the Battery seawall, an official kiosk, the 9/11 Memorial and a ferry labeled "Maybe." No rigorous analysis comparing these opposing approaches is provided.

5. Direction of travel

This reviewer asked in 2025 that direction of travel be chosen on the basis of prevailing winds and elevation. The authors ride north. No wind data are presented. The reviewer notes that the authors chose the direction that ends at the racetrack and suspects this was the entire analysis.

Minor issues

  • No rest days are incorporated. This reviewer requested them in 2025 and requests them again.
  • The manuscript promises "a brewery every night." Several establishments are mentioned without nutritional considerations.
  • The "Why this one" section contains unjustified assertions, including the claim that the towns are better, which is a matter of taste.
  • The short first day is described as respecting the group's preferences. This is the second consecutive proposal in which the authors call their mileage distribution strategic without supporting evidence.
  • The Culinary Institute is described as "possibly the best meal we've had on one of these." The authors have not reported their prior meals with sufficient precision to support a ranking.
  • Drinking sulfur water from a public spring on Day 5 is proposed without IRB approval.

Conclusion

This proposal requires substantial revision before it can be considered viable. The authors must:

  • Choose a week, and state whether horses will be running during it
  • Reduce Day 2 to 65 miles or fewer, or justify the exceedance with something other than the 2025 outcome
  • Reconcile the mileage in the text with the mileage on the map
  • Incorporate a rest day
  • Reserve Amtrak bike space and Saratoga lodging the day dates are set
  • Choose one starting line

The concept has merit. In fairness, this reviewer notes that the concept is the best these authors have submitted, that the Day 5 pass by the Waterford locks, where the Erie Canal departs, is a graceful nod to the withdrawn manuscript, and that this reviewer would very much like to see the finish in Saratoga. The execution, however, is the authors' usual. I recommend rejection in its current form with an invitation to resubmit after addressing these substantial concerns.

Reviewer 2 expects the authors to ignore the mileage ceiling again and to describe the result as legendary.
