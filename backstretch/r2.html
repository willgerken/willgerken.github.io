Referee report

Reviewer 2

Manuscript: "Pedal or Perish Trip #6: Battery to the Backstretch, Lower Manhattan to Saratoga Springs"
Reviewer history: Ohio to Erie (reject), GAP/C&O (accept, minor), this one
Recommendation: Reject and resubmit

This reviewer has seen these authors before. In 2025 this reviewer recommended rejection of their Ohio to Erie proposal. In 2026 the manuscript was assigned to a more agreeable colleague, who accepted the GAP/C&O plan with minor revisions. That colleague has been reassigned. The concerns raised in 2025 are revisited below. Progress has been limited.

1. Excessive Daily Mileage

The proposed mileage distribution remains wildly optimistic:

  • Day 2 proposes 72 miles, exceeding the 65-mile ceiling established in the prior round
  • Day 3 proposes 61 miles immediately afterward, with no recovery day
  • The authors' own route map reports longer distances than the text

This reviewer notes that the 2025 proposal included a 94-mile day. This reviewer has not been told how it went and has chosen not to ask.

2. Unsupported Off-Road Claims

The original manuscript described the route as "about 90%" off-road. Review of the state's own trail pages finds:

  • Approximately 35 consecutive miles of road shoulders between Kingston and Hudson on Day 3
  • A bridge where one prior rider reports traffic "barreling at 50 mph"
  • An earlier draft that crossed the Hudson on a bridge the route never reaches

The revised manuscript now concedes about 70%. The authors have expressed a strong preference for avoiding roads. No evidence is presented that this preference informed the route.

3. Availability Window Mismatch

In 2025 the proposed window was too compressed. The authors have resolved this by not proposing a window at all. The manuscript lists "When: week TBD." In addition:

  • The trip's title depends on racing at Saratoga, and the 2027 race dates are unpublished
  • There is no June racing at Saratoga in 2027
  • A supported group tour of the same route is scheduled for August 2027, competing for the same hotels

A study whose central outcome may not occur during the study period is not a study. It is a hope.

4. Logistical Deficiencies

The transportation logistics continue to suffer from critical flaws:

  • The proposal begins with a drive to Saratoga Springs, followed by a 3.5- to 4-hour train ride, followed by cycling
  • The unspecified "wife" of 2025 has been replaced by Amtrak, which may carry only four bicycles per train
  • At least one co-author's bicycle is described only as "how it gets back from Saratoga"
  • Day 4 relies on a bridge crossing the state has run on a detour since 2024
  • Day 5 relies on a trail the authors concede has "closures with no notice online"

This reviewer considers the wife the more reliable instrument.

5. Methodological Inconsistencies

The authors present multiple contradictory proposals without reconciling their differences:

  • A Buffalo-to-Albany canal route, voted for and then withdrawn
  • Four separate starting points for the present route: a harbor photo, an official kiosk, the 9/11 Memorial and a ferry labeled "Maybe"

No rigorous analysis comparing these opposing approaches is provided.

Minor Issues

  • No rest days are incorporated, despite a prior request
  • Several food establishments are mentioned without nutritional considerations
  • The "Why this one" section contains unjustified assertions, including that the towns are better
  • The mileage distribution is again justified by analogy to a prior trip rather than by evidence
  • The Culinary Institute dinner is ranked against prior meals that were never reported
  • Drinking sulfur water from a public spring is proposed without IRB approval
  • Direction of travel was again chosen without reference to prevailing winds. The authors ride north, which ends at the racetrack. This reviewer notes, with some irritation, that the summer valley winds appear to favor them anyway

Conclusion

This proposal requires substantial revision before it can be considered viable. The authors must:

  • Choose a week, and state whether horses will be running during it
  • Reduce Day 2 to 65 miles or fewer
  • Eliminate or justify the 35-mile road segment
  • Incorporate adequate rest days
  • Reserve Amtrak bike space and Saratoga lodging the day dates are set
  • Choose one starting line

The concept has merit. In fairness, this reviewer notes that it is the best these authors have submitted, and that the Day 5 pass by the Waterford locks, where the Erie Canal departs, is a graceful nod to the withdrawn manuscript. The execution, however, remains fundamentally flawed. I recommend rejection in its current form with an invitation to resubmit after addressing these substantial concerns.

Reviewer 2 expects the authors to ignore the mileage ceiling again.
