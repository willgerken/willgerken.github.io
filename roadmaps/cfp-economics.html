How to add the certificate

What it is

Financial planners help people manage money across investments, taxes, retirement, insurance, and estates. Personal financial advisors earned a median of $105,070 in 2025, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. The six courses satisfy the education requirement for CFP® certification; the exam, experience, and ethics requirements complete it. Open to any major, and full-time on-campus undergraduates add it in myGPS at no additional tuition. Each course needs a C or better. No business courses are needed first; only FIN 359 must come after the others.

The certificate takes six of the plan’s nine electives, and three stay open. Every certificate course lands in an existing elective slot.

Spring start, or three terms left? FIN 350, FIN 357 in spring; ACC 357, ACC 457, FIN 358 in fall; FIN 359 last. Expect a heavier term.

Three things to know

1.
It fits in the electives. Six of nine, with three still open.
2.
Take ACC 357 in a fall. It is the one course that runs in a single term, so a student who reaches senior spring without it cannot finish before graduating (Executive Education afterward costs $895 a course).
3.
Save FIN 359 for last. It is the capstone and runs both terms. Once ACC 357 has its fall, the other courses can go in either term.

After the certificate

  • Students can sit for the CFP exam as soon as their completion is reported. Experience (6,000 hours, or 4,000 through an apprenticeship) and the bachelor’s degree can follow the exam.
  • Licenses depend on the job: Series 65 or 66 for RIA roles; SIE, Series 7, and 63 or 66 for broker-dealers; state life and health for insurance. We recommend the SIE for every student: it needs no sponsoring firm and is not required for the CFP.
  • Get involved: the Finance Society’s Financial Planning Pathway.

To add the certificate: talk with an advisor, then myUK → myGPS → My Program of Study → Request a Major Change → Add a Major, and search for Financial Planning (adds the certificate, not a second major). Details: gatton.uky.edu/certificate-financial-planning, or use the link at the top.
