Emergency

Call 911 first. Give the street address (it is on the fridge and in the family doc, not on this page), then say "Long Lake Township, on the peninsula." Then call Will or Mom.
911 fire · police · medical Grand Traverse Metro Fire and the county sheriff cover the peninsula. Munson Medical Center ER The Traverse City hospital, about 20 minutes. Emergency room is open around the clock. Poison Control 24 hr Anything swallowed that should not have been. 800-222-1222 Smell gas? DTE Everyone outside first, then call. Do not flip switches on the way out. 800-947-5000
Water leakingMain shutoff is in the basement utility room, at the pressure tank. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Then text Will.
Power outThe generator starts on its own in about 30 seconds. If it does not, check the outage map below and text Will; do not touch the transfer switch.
Breaker trippedPanel is in the basement. Flip the one that is half-way, fully off then on. The crock pump outlet is the GFCI at the back of the garage.
Alarm horn in the basementTwo TankAlert boxes: a horn means the septic pump or the outside sump is backed up. Press Silence, stop running water, call Will.
No heatCheck the thermostat is on Heat, then the furnace switch (looks like a light switch on the furnace). Still nothing: Team Bob's, below.
PondDeep water starts a few feet from shore. Life jackets on the paddle boat and kayaks, no exceptions, and nobody on the ice without an adult who checked it.

Monitoring

If an alert lands: MarCELL first (it is the alarm), then Reolink to look, then the generator app if the power is out.

MarCELL cellular Temperature, humidity, power, and the water pucks. Runs on Verizon, so it works with the house internet down. Alarm ranges 40–85°F, 10–70% RH. Also the MarCELL app. Reolink cameras app only Utility room and window cams. Open the Reolink app on your phone for live view; the cameras record to their own memory cards. The Reolink website will show zero devices (that page is only for paid cloud plans). On a laptop, install the Reolink Client from this link. Generac Mobile Link app Generator status, weekly exercise log, and an alert when utility power drops or the generator faults. Set up and live; uses its own cellular link, so it works when the house internet is down. TP-Link Deco app Mesh WiFi. Use the Deco app to see whether the cameras are online, reboot nodes, and check what is connected. Thermostat not yet Smart thermostat waits on the C-wire check. ecobee login here; if it ends up a Nest, use home.nest.com. Set a permanent hold, never a schedule. YoLink move-in Leak sensors plus the auto water shutoff. Decision deferred to move-in; the MarCELL pucks cover the vacant phase. Fish Deeper pond Sonar maps from the Deeper unit, synced from the app after each tow. The draft map is in Around the lake.

House facts

The things you need at the hardware store or on the phone with a contractor.

FurnaceTrane XR95, 95% condensing, two-pipe vent, with condensate pump. Filter 16×25×1, arrow points toward the furnace.
Water softenerKinetico 2060s, non-electric twin tank. Owens Soft Water services it.
GeneratorGenerac standby with a Nexus service-rated transfer switch: whole-house, automatic. Leave the switch in AUTO. The old 10-circuit manual switch is legacy; all toggles stay on LINE.
Sump / crock pumpExterior crock under the faux-rock cover, discharges to the pond. Powered from the back-of-garage GFCI: if the pump is mysteriously dead, reset that outlet first.
High-water alarmsTwo TankAlert panels in the basement: one septic lift, one exterior crock. Push-to-test on each; test during the generator load test.
Septic1,600-gal tank plus pump chamber off the east foundation; pressure-mound field west of the house, long axis east-west. Keep trees and vehicles off it.
Pond aeratorOff for winter, on again in spring. In late summer run it on a timer a few hours midday before going continuous, so a stratified pond does not turn over.
DehumidifierContinuous drain to the basement tub, set at 50%.
CalendarPrincipal Residence Exemption (MI Form 2368) by June 1, 2027. UPS battery every 3 years. Annual well water test. Generator service each summer. Chimney sweep and furnace service each fall.

Township & county

Long Lake Township handles zoning, permits, and taxes. Grand Traverse County handles GIS, health, and emergency alerts.

Long Lake Township Home page, with the Lake Levels link and meeting notices. Township treasurer taxes Summer and winter tax bills, due dates, and where to pay. Property & tax lookup (BS&A) Assessed value, taxable value, and tax history for any parcel in the township. Permits Building, egress window, pole barn, dock. Start here before any contractor does. Zoning & planning R-2 Lake Residential rules: setbacks, accessory buildings, shoreline. County maps (GIS) Parcels, aerials, the septic field location, and wetlands layers. County Health Department Well and septic permits, water sampling, time-of-transfer records. CodeRED alerts sign up County emergency notifications by text. Worth having on both phones. Michigan PRE by 6/1/2027 Principal Residence Exemption: Form 2368 to the township once this is home. Cuts the school operating mills. EGLE State environmental agency. Any boardwalk, dock, or shoreline work near the pond goes through them. Burn permit check Whether open burning is allowed today in Grand Traverse County. Check before the brush pile or a big fire-pit night.

Around the lake

The fun part. Beach and launch keys hang by the garage door; put them back.

Pond depth, draft from the 9/2 sonar tow: mostly 4 to 8 feet, one hole near the west end reads 18 feet. Deep water starts a few feet from shore. The full map gets redone at fall break.
Association beach & launch members Private beach, dock, swim raft, and boat launch about a quarter mile down the road. Kayak carts are in the garage. Golf carts are the neighborhood way to get there.
Park & playground Association park with a ball field, basketball court, playground, and pavilion. Reachable through the woods behind the house without touching a real road.
Lake rules & boat wash Long Lake Association: no-wake rules, invasive species, and what to do with a boat that has been on another lake. Centre Ice Arena hockey The Traverse City rink, about seven minutes away. Public skate and stick-and-puck times. TART Trails bikes Paved and dirt trail maps around Traverse City, including the VASA singletrack. Library (TADL) Traverse Area District Library. Cards, hours, and the rainy-day plan. Moomers ice cream Homemade ice cream on a dairy farm ten minutes away. Regularly voted the best in the country. Non-negotiable stop. Bay beaches Clinch Park and West End downtown, the State Park beach on East Bay. Long Lake is warmer; the bay is bigger. Civic Center & Howe Arena skate County park with the second rink, public skate, a track, and ball fields. Centre Ice is the other rink. Hickory Hills sled · ski City-run hill on the west side: cheap skiing, sledding, and disc golf in summer. Closest snow to the house. Pit Spitters baseball summer Minor-league baseball at Turtle Creek. Fireworks nights and cheap seats. Schooner Manitou Two-hour sails on the bay from downtown. Kids can help raise the sails. Children's museum Great Lakes Children's Museum by the water. Best for the younger end. State Theatre movies Downtown movie house with $1 kids' matinees on weekends. Farmers market Sat Sara Hardy market downtown, Saturday mornings May through October, Wednesdays in summer. The Village at GT Commons The old asylum turned into shops, bakeries, and trails. Good rainy afternoon, good bike destination. Recreation Passport state parks The state-park sticker for the car, added at plate renewal. Needed for the State Park beach, Interlochen, and every boat launch on state land. Fishing license Anyone 17 and up needs a Michigan license, even in the pond. Kids under 17 fish free. Bass and bluegill are in there.

Eat & drink

The grown-up list. Two wine trails, the two breweries that are also a night out, and the place on our own lake.

Boone's Long Lake Inn our lake Steaks and the Friday fish fry, five minutes away on Long Lake. The default when nobody wants to cook. Farm Club Leelanau Farm, bakery, brewery, and restaurant on the Leelanau Trail. Bike there from Suttons Bay or drive 25 minutes. Kids run the fields. Hop Lot Brewing Suttons Bay Brewery in the woods with fire pits, a big lawn, and a real menu. Kids welcome outside. Leelanau wine trail The 20-plus wineries between Suttons Bay and Northport, with the tasting-pass deals. Old Mission wine trail The nine wineries up the peninsula east of town. Pair with the lighthouse drive. Restaurant guide Tourism bureau's dining list, filterable by neighborhood and type. Restaurant Week is in late winter.

Seasons & fun

Fall color peaks up here around the second week of October. One leaf site, one of everything else.

Fall things to do in TC cider · pumpkins Color tour routes, pumpkin patches, and cider mills from the tourism bureau. Fall color map leaf site The one leaf site: county-level peak forecast, updated through the season. Pick the drive-up weekend from this. M-22 scenic drive The Leelanau loop past Sleeping Bear, Glen Arbor, and Leland. Best color drive within an hour. Sleeping Bear Dunes Dune climb, Pierce Stocking scenic drive, Empire bluff. Forty minutes west. Jacob's Farm corn maze Corn maze, cider, donuts, and live music on the edge of town. Friske Farm Market Orchard, u-pick apples, and the pie counter up US-31 in Ellsworth. Salmon run Sept – Oct DNR weekly fishing report. Kings run up the Boardman and the Betsie every fall; go watch them at the weir. Northern lights aurora NOAA 30-minute aurora forecast. On a strong night they show over the lake; dark sky is one of the perks up here. Mt. Holiday ski · tubing The little hill on the east side of town: night skiing, tubing, and lessons. Ten minutes. Crystal Mountain The real ski hill, about 40 minutes southwest. Also mountain biking and a water park in summer. VASA trail & race Groomed cross-country ski trails and fat-bike loops in the Pere Marquette forest. The Vasa race is in February. Snowmobile trails Tri-County Snowmobile Alliance trail maps and grooming reports. Great Wolf Lodge rainy day Indoor water park in town. Day passes exist; check before promising the kids. Downtown TC events Friday night live, the tree lighting, Restaurant Week, and what is on this weekend. Interlochen concerts Summer concert series and student performances all year, 20 minutes south. Mission Point Lighthouse Old Mission Drive the length of Old Mission Peninsula to the lighthouse on the 45th parallel. Beach, trails, and a lighthouse to climb. Fishtown, Leland Working fishing shanties, smoked fish, and the ferry to the Manitou islands. On the M-22 loop. Elk Rapids & Torch Lake sandbar North on US-31. The Torch Lake sandbar is the summer day the kids will talk about; launch from Elk Rapids or Alden. Mackinac Island day trip Two hours to the ferry, then bikes, fudge, and the fort. No cars on the island. Timberlee Hills tubing winter Big tubing hill 15 minutes west. Easier than skiing with a mixed-age crew. Boyne Mountain ski · water park Bigger resort an hour north: skiing, the indoor water park, and the summer zip line. National Cherry Festival July Air show, parades, and the whole town on the bay for a week around the Fourth.
