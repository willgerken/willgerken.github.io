Apps

All four use the family login. If an alert comes in: MarCELL first, then the cameras, then the generator app if the power is out.

MarCELL the alarm Temperature, humidity, power, and the water pucks. It has its own cell connection, so it keeps working when the house internet is down.
Reolink cameras app only Live view is in the Reolink app. The cameras record to their own cards. Don't bother with the Reolink website, it shows nothing.
Mobile Link generator Generator status and the weekly exercise log. It pings when the power drops. Own cell connection too. Deco WiFi If the cameras go dark, open Deco first. It shows whether the house internet is up and lets you reboot the mesh.

If something breaks

Power outThe generator starts itself in about 30 seconds. Check the Consumers outage map to see if it's the whole peninsula. Leave the transfer switch alone.
Water leakShutoff is in the basement at the pressure tank. Clockwise till it stops.
Horn in the basementOne of the two TankAlert boxes. The septic pump or the outside crock is backed up. Hit Silence and stop running water.
Crock pump not runningReset the GFCI outlet at the back of the garage first. That's what feeds it.
Breaker trippedPanel is in the garage. Find the one sitting halfway, push it fully off, then on. If the panel looks fine, check the gray EmerGen box: ten press-to-reset buttons, and every rocker stays on LINE.
No waterCheck the well pump breaker first, then look at the pressure gauge on the tank in the basement. If it's at zero with the breaker on, Cluff.
No heatThermostat on Heat, then the switch on the side of the furnace (looks like a light switch). Still nothing, Team Bob's.
Smell gasEverybody outside, then DTE at 800-947-5000.
Internet downSpectrum outage page. The alarm and generator apps keep working without it.

House notes

The stuff you need at the hardware store or on the phone with somebody.

Furnace filter16×25×1, arrow toward the furnace. Trane XR95.
SoftenerKinetico 2060, no electricity. Each visit: refill the tube in the salt tank with half a cup of citric acid per gallon of water (the previous owner's recipe, written on the red lid). Poke the salt with a broom handle so it isn't bridged.
GeneratorGenerac, whole house, automatic. Switch stays on AUTO. The old manual switch in the basement is dead; all its toggles stay on LINE.
Crock pumpOutside under the fake rock, pumps to the pond. Fed by the GFCI at the back of the garage.
TankAlertsTwo boxes in the basement: septic lift and the crock. Push-to-test on each.
SepticTank and pump chamber off the east side of the house, mound field west of the house. Nothing parked or planted on it.
Pond aeratorOff for winter, on in spring. Late summer: a few hours a day on a timer before leaving it on all the time.
DehumidifierDrains to the basement tub. 50% when we're there, 45% when we leave.
Every yearGenerator service in summer. Chimney sweep and furnace service in fall. Well water test. UPS battery every three years. Principal Residence Exemption, Form 2368, by June 1, 2027.
PondDepth map. Drops to 4 to 6 feet a few steps from shore, one 20-foot hole at the west end. Life jackets on the boats. Nobody on the ice without one of us checking it.
