The lake & town

Association beach & launch members Private beach, dock, swim raft, and boat launch a quarter mile down the road. Kayak carts are in the garage. Everybody golf-carts there.
Park & playground Ball field, basketball court, playground, pavilion. You can get there through the woods behind the house.
Lake rules & boat wash No-wake rules and what to do with a boat that's been on another lake. Moomers ice cream Five minutes. Not optional. Centre Ice Arena hockey Seven minutes. Public skate and stick-and-puck times. Civic Center & Howe Arena skate The other rink, plus a track and ball fields. TART Trails bikes Paved and dirt trail maps, including the VASA singletrack. Library Traverse Area District Library. The rainy-day plan. Bay beaches Clinch Park and West End downtown, the State Park beach on East Bay. Long Lake is warmer, the bay is bigger. Hickory Hills sled · ski City hill on the west side. Cheap skiing, sledding, disc golf in summer. Closest snow to the house. State Theatre movies Downtown. $1 kids' matinees on weekends. Farmers market Sat Saturday mornings May through October, Wednesdays in summer. The Village at GT Commons The old asylum, now shops and bakeries and trails. Good gray afternoon. Children's museum By the water. For the younger end. Pit Spitters summer Minor-league baseball at Turtle Creek. Fireworks nights, cheap seats. Schooner Manitou Two-hour sails on the bay from downtown. Kids help with the sails. Downtown events What's on this weekend. Recreation Passport state parks The state-park sticker, added at plate renewal. Needed for the State Park beach, Interlochen, and every state boat launch. Fishing license 17 and up needs one, even in the pond. Kids fish free. Bass and bluegill are in there.

Eat & drink

Boone's Long Lake Inn our lake Steaks and the Friday fish fry, five minutes away. The default when nobody wants to cook. Farm Club Leelanau Farm, bakery, brewery, restaurant. Bike there from Suttons Bay or drive 25 minutes. Kids run the fields. Hop Lot Brewing Suttons Bay Brewery in the woods with fire pits and a big lawn. Kids welcome outside. Leelanau wine trail Twenty-some wineries between Suttons Bay and Northport. Old Mission wine trail Nine wineries up the peninsula east of town. Pair with the lighthouse drive. Restaurant guide The tourism bureau's list. Restaurant Week is late winter.

Seasons & day trips

Fall color peaks around the second week of October.

Fall in TC cider · pumpkins Color routes, pumpkin patches, cider mills. Fall color map County-level peak forecast. Pick the weekend from this. M-22 The Leelanau loop past Sleeping Bear, Glen Arbor, and Leland. Best color drive within an hour. Sleeping Bear Dunes Dune climb, Pierce Stocking drive, Empire Bluff. Forty minutes west. Fishtown, Leland Fishing shanties, smoked fish, the Manitou ferry. On the M-22 loop. Mission Point Lighthouse Drive the length of Old Mission to the tip. Beach, trails, a lighthouse to climb. Jacob's Farm corn maze Corn maze, cider, donuts, live music on the edge of town. Friske Farm Market U-pick apples and the pie counter, up US-31 in Ellsworth. Salmon run Sept – Oct Kings run up the Boardman and the Betsie every fall. Go watch them at the weir. Northern lights NOAA's 30-minute aurora forecast. On a good night they show over the lake. Mt. Holiday ski · tubing The little hill on the east side of town. Night skiing, tubing, lessons. Ten minutes. Timberlee Hills tubing Big tubing hill 15 minutes west. Easier than skiing with a mixed-age crew. Crystal Mountain The real ski hill, 40 minutes southwest. Mountain biking and a water park in summer. Boyne Mountain An hour north. Skiing, the indoor water park, the summer zip line. VASA trail Groomed cross-country and fat-bike loops. The race is in February. Snowmobile trails Trail maps and grooming reports. Great Wolf Lodge rainy day Indoor water park in town. Day passes exist; check before promising the kids. Interlochen Summer concerts and student performances all year. Twenty minutes south. Torch Lake sandbar North on US-31. Launch from Elk Rapids or Alden. The summer day the kids will talk about. Mackinac Island day trip Two hours to the ferry, then bikes, fudge, the fort. Cherry Festival July Air show, parades, the whole town on the bay for the week of the Fourth.

The drive

Lexington to the lake is three states of road conditions.

GoKY Kentucky The first hour. OHGO Ohio I-75 through Ohio. MiDrive Michigan Closures and plow cams for the last three hours. Cherry Capital Airport When the van isn't the answer. Traverse Ticker Local news and road projects. TCAPS The school district. Calendar and enrollment for the move.
